About Namao

Namao is a personal-use downloader for YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X (Twitter), and Instagram. Paste a link, and it reads every quality the video was actually uploaded in — from its original resolution down to whatever the source offers — plus an audio-only MP3 option.

Downloads run on the server you host it on, through yt-dlp and FFmpeg. No account, no ads, no paid third-party API.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do downloads get saved?

Wherever your browser saves downloads by default. When a job finishes, Namao offers the verified file as a normal browser download.

Can I download audio only?

Yes — pick the "Audio only" row in the quality list. It is converted to MP3 on the server.

Why do some videos take a while?

Higher resolutions are downloaded as separate video and audio streams and merged by FFmpeg, then checked before they are offered to you. The progress bar shows the real state of the job and you can cancel it at any time.

Does it store my videos anywhere?

Only temporarily. Each job gets its own folder, which is deleted when it is cancelled, when it fails, and automatically after the retention period (one hour by default).

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X/Twitter, and Instagram. Anything else is rejected with a clear message.

How it works

  1. 1
    Paste a link

    Copy a video URL from YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X, or Instagram and drop it in the box above.

  2. 2
    Hit Fetch

    Namao reads the video's title, thumbnail, and every quality it was actually uploaded in.

  3. 3
    Pick a quality

    Compare estimated file sizes and choose a resolution, or grab audio only.

  4. 4
    Download

    Watch real progress, cancel if you like, and save the finished file with your browser.
