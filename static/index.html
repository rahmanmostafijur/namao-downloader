        About Namao

        A self-hosted downloader for personal use. No accounts, no ads, no paid downloading API.

        Paste a link and Namao reads every quality the video was actually uploaded in, plus an audio-only MP3 option. Downloads run on the server you host it on, through yt-dlp and FFmpeg. A file is only offered after it has been checked, and it is never reported as finished while it is still being merged.

        Questions

        Where do downloads get saved?

        Wherever your browser saves downloads. When a job finishes, Namao offers the verified file as a normal browser download. The desktop app can also copy it into a folder you choose.

        Can I download audio only?

        Yes. Pick the audio row in the quality list. It is converted to MP3 on the server.

        Why do some videos take a while?

        Higher resolutions are downloaded as separate video and audio streams and merged by FFmpeg, then checked before they are offered to you. The progress bar shows the real state of the job.

        Does it store my videos?

        Only temporarily. Each job gets its own folder, which is deleted when it is cancelled, when it fails, and automatically after the retention period (one hour by default).

        Why is a quality missing?

        Namao only lists what the source offers for that video. A resolution that is not available is never silently swapped for another.

        Good to know

        • Single, public, non-live videos only. Namao does not bypass DRM, logins, paywalls or age gates.
        • Platforms sometimes throttle or block a server’s address. When that happens Namao says so instead of failing silently.
        • Only download videos you have the right to save, and respect each platform’s terms.
