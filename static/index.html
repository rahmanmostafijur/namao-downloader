About Namao

Namao is a personal-use downloader for YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X (Twitter), and Instagram. Paste a link, and it reads every quality the video was actually uploaded in — from its original resolution down to 360p — plus an audio-only MP3 option.

Everything runs locally on your own machine through yt-dlp. No account, no ads, and nothing is ever uploaded to a third-party server — the video goes straight from the source to your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do downloads get saved?

Wherever your browser saves downloads by default — Namao just hands the file to the browser, the same as any other download link.

Can I download audio only?

Yes — pick the "Audio only" row in the quality list. It comes out as MP3 if ffmpeg is installed, or M4A otherwise.

Do I need ffmpeg?

Only for 1080p+ merged video and MP3 conversion. Without it, Namao still works but is limited to progressive (pre-merged) formats.

Does it store my videos anywhere?

No. Each download uses a fresh temporary folder that's deleted the moment the file reaches your browser.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X/Twitter, and Instagram. Anything else is rejected with a clear message.

How it works

  1. 1
    Paste a link

    Copy a video URL from YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X, or Instagram and drop it in the box above.

  2. 2
    Hit Fetch

    Namao reads the video's title, thumbnail, and every quality it was actually uploaded in.

  3. 3
    Pick a quality

    Compare estimated file sizes and choose a resolution, or grab audio only.

  4. 4
    Download

    Your browser handles the save — no extra windows, no redirects.
