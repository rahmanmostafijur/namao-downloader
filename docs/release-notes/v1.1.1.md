## Namao for Android 1.1.1

**Facebook Reels, Instagram Stories and short links now work, and error messages say what actually went wrong.**

### What changed
- **Facebook Reels** (`facebook.com/reel/…`, `/reels/…`) were wrongly rejected as "not supported". They now work. The same bug blocked YouTube clips and `vm.tiktok.com` links; fixed too.
- **Short and share links** (`fb.watch`, Facebook `/share/…`, Instagram `/share/…`, `instagr.am`) are followed safely to the real video: HTTPS only, at most 5 hops, and only to the same platform's own hosts.
- **Honest error messages.** "This link is not supported yet." appears only for links from other sites. Otherwise you now see what happened: unavailable or no access, a login needed, the platform temporarily restricting access, or a link that could not be opened. Technical details are never shown.
- **Instagram session (optional).** Most public Instagram posts and Reels download with no login. For something you are allowed to see that needs a login, **Settings > Instagram session** lets you import your own `cookies.txt` (exported from your browser while logged in). It stays on your phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore, is used only for Instagram links, and can be removed at any time. Namao never asks for your password. Sessions expire and Instagram can end one at any time; a session Instagram no longer accepts never breaks public posts.
- Instagram **Stories** are now recognised (they need an imported session).

### Requirements
- Android 10 (API 29) or newer, 64-bit ARM, and an internet connection.

### Known limits
- Facebook private, removed or login-walled videos all look the same to the downloader, so Namao reports "unavailable or you don't have access". Facebook sessions are not supported.
- Instagram does not say why it refuses a post (private, removed, or rate-limited), so Namao reports that Instagram didn't return it.
- Tested on an Android 16 (API 36) emulator and with automated tests (426): real downloads of a Facebook Reel and public Instagram Reels, and import, storage, removal and rejection of Instagram sessions using synthetic files. **Not tested:** a real `fb.watch`/share link, a real logged-in Instagram session, TikTok, X, or a physical device.
- Personal use only. Namao does not bypass DRM, logins or paywalls.

### Update
Install `namao-android.apk` over the old one; history is kept because every release is signed with the same key. (The in-app updater can also do it.)
